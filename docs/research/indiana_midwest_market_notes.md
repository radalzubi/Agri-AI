# Indiana and Midwest Market Notes

Updated: 2026-03-23

## Core Reality

This is not a generic Midwest agriculture play.
It is a specialty-crop and local-to-regional supply coordination play.

If the company markets itself as a broad agricultural platform, it will overstate TAM and confuse the actual wedge.

## What Growers Are Already Doing

Purdue's 2024–2025 producer survey showed:

- 99% of respondents had a seasonal production plan
- 78% had a marketing plan
- 86% said market outlets influence production choices

This matters because it means the problem is not a total lack of planning.
The problem is fragmented planning across buyers, growers, and aggregator operations.

## What Growers Say Is Hard

The most important barriers reported in Purdue's survey were:

- disease pressure
- reliable labor

Planning software is not their first stated pain.
This is a critical insight: growers will not pull demand for this product.
The operator or aggregator must be the entry point.

## Why the Midwest Is Tricky

- much of Midwest agriculture is commodity-focused, not specialty-crop coordination
- local produce demand is seasonal while many institutional buyers need continuity
- many food hubs and local sourcing programs are grant-backed and budget constrained
- trust and relationships still drive procurement and commitment decisions

## Indiana-Specific Implication

Indiana is a reasonable pilot geography because:

- there is enough vegetable and specialty-crop activity to test the workflow
- Purdue extension and local food networks provide access paths
- institutional and regional procurement programs already exist

Indiana is not large enough to justify a broad platform thesis on its own.
It is a proving ground, not the final market case.

## Best Local Beachhead

Prioritize operators that already coordinate multiple growers for recurring buyers:

- food hubs
- regional aggregators
- produce distributors with local programs
- statewide or regional local food coordinators

Avoid starting with:

- individual direct-market farms
- pure row-crop operations
- buyers without recurring local procurement volume
