# Competitor Landscape

## Category Truth

Agri-AI is not entering an empty category.

The market already has:

- farm management systems
- food hub and local food commerce software
- buyer-side sourcing tools
- spreadsheets and manual coordination

## Real Competitor Categories

### Food hub and local food software

- Local Line
- Local Food Marketplace

These tools already manage multi-vendor orders, fulfillment, and operational workflow.

### Buyer-side sourcing tools

- Forager
- Local Line buyer workflows

These tools already touch local procurement and supplier coordination.

### Farm and produce systems

- FarmERP
- produce ERP and traceability tools

These platforms cover operations and record-keeping, but they are not the right proof point for Agri-AI's first wedge.

### Manual workflow

The largest real competitor remains:

- spreadsheets
- email
- text
- phone calls

## What Existing Tools Miss

- pre-season demand-to-grower commitment planning
- shortage and overage scenario planning before planting
- lightweight weekly fill-risk visibility tied to expected supply

## Strategic Implication

Agri-AI should not try to replace every adjacent tool.
It should own one missing workflow cleanly:
planning before commitments are locked.
