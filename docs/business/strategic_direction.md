# Strategic Direction

## Recommended Sequence

Agri-AI should build in three layers, but only one of them belongs in the first phase.

### Phase 1

Pre-season planning layer for aggregators and regional produce operators.

Objective:

- prove operators will pay for better demand-to-grower coordination

### Phase 2

Structured commitment workflow between buyers and grower networks.

Objective:

- move from planning visibility into stronger commitments

### Phase 3

Financial or risk products, only if the data quality and commitment volume justify them.

Objective:

- monetize the data and workflow position after trust is established

## What Not to Do Early

- do not start as a lender
- do not start as a marketplace
- do not claim a category-defining AI platform before the workflow is proven

## Strategic Wedge

The company should not market itself as infrastructure for all agriculture.

The sharpest wedge is:

planning infrastructure for buyer-facing specialty-crop operators that need to align grower commitments with recurring demand.

## Commercial Logic

This wedge is strong because:

- the pain is measurable
- the operator already coordinates multiple stakeholders
- the operator can become a multiplier for grower-side usage
- the workflow creates valuable proprietary data over time

## Decision Rule

Any roadmap item that does not improve one of these should be deprioritized:

- fill rate
- emergency buy reduction
- overage reduction
- operator planning speed
- buyer-demand visibility before planting
